Research

Active
Linearly Polarised Dielectric-Filled Waveguide
Design, simulation and fabrication of a linearly polarised waveguide antenna with dielectric filling. Design to be used as a benchmark for future novel antenna designs.
Waveguide Linear Pol. Dielectric CST
Up next
Circularly Polarised Waveguide Antenna
Evolution of the LP design to achieve circular polarisation — enabling improved coupling efficiency and reduced standing wave artefacts in thermoacoustic imaging scenarios.
Waveguide Circular Pol. CP Design
In progress
E-Field Characterisation
Experimental near-field and far-field mapping of the waveguide antenna's electric field distribution — validating simulation models against measured radiation patterns.
Measurement Near-field VNA Field Probe
Planned
Thermoacoustic Imaging Results
Using the characterised waveguide antennas to generate and reconstruct thermoacoustic images — demonstrating the system's capability as a novel imaging modality.
